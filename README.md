## Publications

<table>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

#### [Real-Time Vehicle Detection and License Plate Recognition for Smart Traffic Management](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-27669-8_11)

<sub><b>Springer · Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, Vol. 2011 · ICCTRDA 2025 · pp. 115–130</b></sub>

A real-time traffic surveillance system for smart cities. **YOLOv8** detects and tracks vehicles in live video, and the **Gemini** vision-language model reads license plates directly from the detected frames, removing the need for a separate OCR stage. The system reaches **97.2% detection accuracy** at a stable **30 FPS**, with an architecture designed to scale across city camera networks.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td valign="top">

#### [IoT-Based Deep Learning Model for Sustainable Waste Management System](https://jisem-journal.com/index.php/journal/article/view/2455)

<sub><b>Journal of Information Systems Engineering and Management (JISEM)</b></sub>

An end-to-end smart waste system built for fast-growing Indian cities. Bins fitted with **ultrasonic sensors and GPS** track fill levels and trigger a pickup once they pass 80% capacity. Waste is first separated by moisture content, then a **ResNet-50** model sorts dry waste into categories such as metal, plastic, glass, cardboard and textiles. Each stream is routed to recycling, composting or waste-to-energy, cutting landfill dependency and raising recycling rates.

</td>
</tr>
